### Strategic decision making

*Source: Observed. I nominated these focus areas, and each was then checked against the record of my AI working sessions. I show only the findings that record supports.*

**Business model before build.** Across at least four separate product concepts, I completed a business model and risk analysis before any deeper development. I let a concept earn further investment through research, pilots and explicit go or no go gates.

**Technology stacks chosen on cost, scale and fit.** I weigh platform choices and I do not default to them. I compare managed platforms such as Vercel and Supabase with the major clouds (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and with existing hosting, and I treat running cost at scale as a driver of the architecture. Typical outcomes: I choose serverless functions over always on servers for low volume workloads, and I choose built in capabilities over paid third party services where those services would inflate cost as usage grows.

**Advice shaped to the client.** I start from the client's existing platform, not from my preferred stack. I justify migration advice on both price and strategy, and I stage it with clear triggers for when a move becomes worthwhile, so I never push a client into a rebuild they do not yet need.

**Security as a design input.** I put AI generated code through a security review before release. In my working sessions I rotated exposed keys, restricted API keys, separated admin and public codebases for security reasons, threat modelled payment flows, and set two factor authentication as a baseline requirement.

**Lean footprint and verified standards.** I audit my products to remove what they do not need. I research industry standards, licence terms and the health of open source tools before I commit, and I change my plans when that research contradicts them.

**AI as a decision partner, not an oracle.** I use AI to research options, compare trade offs and stress test my conclusions. I challenge and correct its proposals when they do not fit the situation, and I cross check important readings across more than one model.

**Commitment to innovation.** I apply formal innovation management practice, aligned to the ISO 56001 standard, to my client work, and I maintain a steady pipeline of new product concepts.
